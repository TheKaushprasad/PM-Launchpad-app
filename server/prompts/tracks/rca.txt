- Score 5 (Bar-Raiser): Formulated testable hypotheses with explicit expected signals before requesting data; systematically eliminated non-causes; isolated the exact root cause with rigorous causal logic.

4. COMMUNICATION (communication)

- Score 3 (Adequate): Communicated clearly and logically, but occasionally rambled or required prompting to summarize findings.

5. FINAL RECOMMENDATION (synthesis)
- Score 1 (Very Weak): Unclear root cause conclusion; proposed generic solutions without addressing the verified breakdown.
