2. STRUCTURED THINKING (framework)

- Score 5 (Bar-Raiser): Masterful user-centered framework: distinct, non-obvious user segments → acute, prioritized user pain points → differentiated 10x solutions → rigorous multidimensional prioritization matrix.

3. ANALYSIS & REASONING (analyticalRigor)

- Score 3 (Adequate): Prioritized pain points and solutions with defined criteria, but evaluation lacked nuance regarding user behavior, counter-incentives, or edge cases.
- Score 5 (Bar-Raiser): Deep product empathy combined with rigorous prioritization logic and trade-offs; addressed second-order behavioral effects, user friction points, and edge cases; articulated why alternative solutions fall short.

4. COMMUNICATION (communication)
- Score 1 (Very Weak): Dry, robotic listing of features; poor storytelling; failed to paint a vivid picture of the user experience.
- Score 3 (Adequate): Clear, structured explanations of features and workflows, but occasionally verbose or lacking storytelling flair.

5. FINAL RECOMMENDATION (synthesis)
- Score 1 (Very Weak): Vague summary; failed to pick a single winning solution; no metrics or validation roadmap.
- Score 3 (Adequate): Recommended a concrete solution and stated standard engagement/adoption metrics, but lacked counter-metrics or MVP rollout phasing.
